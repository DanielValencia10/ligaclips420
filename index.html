LIGACLIPS420

LigaClips420 is a personal desktop tool used by the creator of the LigaClips420 channel to edit short gaming clips (GTA 6) with subtitles, credits to the original streamer and audio filtering, and to send them as drafts to the creator's own TikTok account and YouTube channel.

The tool runs only on the creator's computer. It is not offered to the public and it does not collect data from other people.
